Game Shelf

Two small games for the iPad. No images, no audio files, no libraries: every pixel and every note is generated when the page opens. Add either one to your Home Screen for fullscreen play.

CafetalGrow coffee on a mountain farm, dry and roast it, and serve the villagers at your roadside café.farm sim · chill
LeñaTap the tree, sell the wood, hire a crew, build a sawmill, and plant new forests for acorns.incremental · idle
Progress is saved in your browser, per game.
